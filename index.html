Our Story

Born from a Mother's Love

Tiny Bliss was founded in 2016 by Sarah Mitchell, a certified pediatric dermatologist and mother of three, who couldn't find a salon she truly trusted for her own babies.

She built a space where every product is dermatologist-approved, every specialist is trained in infant care, and every moment is designed to feel like a hug.

🌿
Only Organic Products

Every product we use is certified organic, hypoallergenic, and free from harsh chemicals.

👩‍⚕️
