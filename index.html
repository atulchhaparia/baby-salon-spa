Our Story

Born from a Mother's Love

Tiny Bliss was founded in 2016 by Dr. Anjali Deshpande, a certified pediatric dermatologist and mother of two from Kothrud, Pune, who couldn't find a salon she truly trusted for her own babies.

She built a space where every product is dermatologist-approved, every specialist is trained in infant care, and every moment is designed to feel like a warm Pune welcome.
